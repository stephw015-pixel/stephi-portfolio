About

I’m Stephanie, a London-based creative director, motion designer and illustrator. I build visual systems, campaign worlds and motion-led stories for brands, apps and digital products.

Growing up across five countries shaped how I work: I notice tone, context and what helps people connect. That shows up in the care behind the visuals, the pacing, and the story.

Alongside client work, I’m building my own tools and experiments where creativity, AI and interaction meet.
