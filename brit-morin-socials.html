Goal

Turn Brit Morin's founder, VC, and future-of-tech ideas into fast, readable, platform-native social videos that could hold attention in a crowded Instagram feed.

The work needed to feel immediate and useful while keeping enough personality and polish to feel ownable.

What I did

I supported the social output through editing, visual direction, captioning, and AI-assisted workflows, using Claude Code, Krea AI, and Creative Cloud to shape video generation, production flow, and content polish.

Scope

  • Short-form video editing for Instagram-first social content
  • Visual direction for pacing, framing, cover style, and graphic rhythm
  • Content polish across educational, founder, AI, and future-of-tech themes
  • AI workflow support across Claude Code, Krea AI, and Creative Cloud for faster ideation, generation, and delivery
  • Public-facing social work linked through Brit Morin's Instagram profile
